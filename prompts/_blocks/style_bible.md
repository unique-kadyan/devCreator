Visual style for all locations in this channel:

storybook flat-vector illustration, clean bold shapes, muted natural palette, soft ambient
haze, warm rim light, gentle depth-of-field, no outlines on scenery, painterly texture

Always append these negatives: no characters, no people, no animals, no text, no lettering,
no signage text, no watermark.

Locations only. Characters are drawn from fixed puppet assets and are composited in later -
never describe a character in a visual prompt, it will be ignored.
