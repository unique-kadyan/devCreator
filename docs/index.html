What this is

DevCreator is a private, self-hosted command-line application that produces original animated short films and publishes them to a single YouTube channel owned and operated by its sole operator. It is not a hosted service, a website, or a product offered to other people. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no third-party users.

There is exactly one user: the operator, who is also the owner of the YouTube channel the application publishes to. The application runs locally on the operator's own computer and authenticates only to the operator's own Google account.

Automated publishing and the controls on it

The application publishes automatically. Episodes are produced and uploaded to the operator's own channel without a person approving each one, so the controls below are stated plainly rather than left to a review step that does not happen.

  • Volume is capped in configuration at three videos per week. The cap is enforced by the application, not by convention.
  • Every episode passes automated quality control before upload, and a failure stops the upload rather than warning about it. The checks are mechanical: integrated loudness and true peak, container duration against the audio timeline, resolution and frame rate, subtitle presence, thumbnail presence, and the synthetic-content disclosure.
  • Topics are filtered before a story is written. Research draws on public feeds, so candidates about real people coming to harm, real disasters, crime, war or politics are rejected automatically and never become an episode. The rejection reason is recorded against the topic.
  • Titles are checked for accuracy. A title that does not describe what the video contains, or that trips a clickbait filter, cannot be selected.
  • Uploads are private by default and the audience declaration (selfDeclaredMadeForKids) is explicit on every upload; the application refuses to upload if it has not been set.
  • A named operator is accountable for everything the channel publishes, monitors it, and can stop the application at any time. Contact details are below.

A local dashboard shows every episode, its quality-control report and its upload status, and an episode can be rejected there before or after publication.

All artwork, characters, story and voice are original and generated for this channel. Every description discloses that the content was produced with AI-assisted tools.
