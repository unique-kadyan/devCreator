5. Automated publishing and the limits on it

The application publishes to the operator's channel automatically, without a person approving each episode. The following limits are enforced by the application itself:

  • A ceiling of three published videos per week.
  • Automated quality control on every episode before upload. A failed check stops the upload; it does not merely record a warning.
  • Automatic rejection of research topics concerning real people coming to harm, real disasters, crime, war or politics.
  • An explicit audience declaration on every upload. The application refuses to upload when it has not been set.

The operator remains responsible for everything the channel publishes, monitors the channel, and can halt the application at any time.
